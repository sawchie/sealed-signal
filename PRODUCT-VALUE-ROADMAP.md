# PokeScratch: useful enough to return to

## Shipped in this update

- A rewritten About page: plain collector language, Alex as a public pen name, no invented career, collection history, credentials, or firsthand tests. The owner's personal information is not published.
- Pack Planner: a working set-by-set opening-budget comparison, using existing active catalog records and sourced pack counts. Editable unit prices, order shipping, merchandise tax, whole-unit quantities, total packs, total spend, leftover budget, and a shortlist. Outputs follow the existing display-currency choice; inputs are explicitly USD.
- Market versus retail reference modes, source dates, older-snapshot labels, specification links, and explicit exclusions for unreleased/undated/count-unknown records. No stock, pull-rate, resale-return or optimal mixed-basket claims.
- Entry links on the main catalog, product detail pages, About, Tools, and sitemap. Catalog card design and existing collection/pricing behavior preserved.

## Bigger direction

Make PokeScratch the place to decide what to buy, not another copy of a marketplace price list. A useful service and original analysis matter more than an impressive-sounding owner biography.

1. **Format decisions, backed by evidence.** The planner is the first working slice. Next: richer exact-edition comparisons, sourced accessory/promo differences, and a small number of genuinely researched set hubs. Avoid generating hundreds of near-identical set pages.
2. **A real market notebook.** Publish short periodic analyses from stored observations: which comparable products changed, by how much, over which dates, and with what data gaps. Only compare like-for-like provider/variant histories. No invented historical chart or claims about what caused a move.
3. **A collector's buy log.** Extend existing collection purchases with optional condition, seller, and receipt notes kept private. Separate paid cost from hypothetical market value and realized sale proceeds. Before adding cloud accounts or receipt storage, settle privacy and backup requirements.
4. **Field-tested buying guides.** Obtain actual packaging photos and observations from willing collectors or the operator, with permission. Turn those into exact-variant checks and practical examples. Code and AI drafting cannot substitute for firsthand evidence that does not exist.

## Quality gates

- Calculations must be reproducible from the displayed inputs; missing prices and pack counts are never zero.
- Every reused price retains its source and date. Retail reference is not an in-stock offer.
- Do not imply a pseudonym has a fabricated résumé or a fictional story is a real person's history.
- Native labels, keyboard controls, mobile overflow checks, and bounded independent review before release.
- No fake reviews, traffic, community activity, price history, or AdSense approval claims.
