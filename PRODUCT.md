# PokeScratch

An independent Pokémon TCG sealed-product comparison tool for collectors checking a retail shelf price against an attributed market estimate. Catalog browsing is for fast comparison; product detail is for the fee-adjusted buy check and source inspection. The site does not sell inventory or promise retailer availability.

## Current commitments

- Preserve D1 administration, original product IDs/URLs, inactive tombstones, and the centralized calculation/recommendation thresholds.
- Keep gross retail spread distinct from net profit after the configured selling costs.
- Never invent retail prices, market quotes, sales samples, release dates, or availability. Unknown fields stay unknown.
- Current catalog: 431 exact-product records, including all 64 established records. Current market import: September 11, 2026, TCGplayer Market Price via TCGCSV; snapshots, not a live feed or individual eBay sales comps.
- User requested removal of public methodology content. The former route redirects to the catalog; private research documentation and individual price provenance remain.
- Product photos are matched by TCGplayer product ID, locally stored and cropped. Source mapping is centralized. Existing user-supplied authorization is the basis for asset use; not a claim that all web imagery is unrestricted.

## Approved visual direction

The user selected Concept 01 (Arcade After Dark): navy ground, restrained purple/cyan accents, large real product photos, four-column desktop gallery, clear names and large paired retail/market values. Signal colors remain aligned with recommendation logic. Keep smaller layouts practical and avoid new dashboard widgets.

Concept 04's seated mascot is requested at high resolution, mirrored to face left, without adjacent lettering. Two built-in image-generation attempts failed during output screening. The prior pixel mascot is deliberately retained until a suitable replacement can be produced; this request is not complete.
